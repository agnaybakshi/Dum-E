# CAD Details
Most of the structure is credited to SG90 mini robot arm S9 by FrN (Robot Arm for everyone!)
by Francis128 is licensed under the Creative Commons - Attribution - Non-Commercial license.

The custom base is designed to replace the native geared base yaw joint for a direct servo driven yaw. 

The project requires three MG90s and two SG90 servos where the MG90s are used for the base yaw, lower pitch and middle pitch joints while the SG90 are used for the upper pitch joint and the gripper end effector. 

PLA and ABS are advised for structural components and gears respectively.

4mm steel balls are used in the base to make the yaw motion smoother.

M2 & M2.5 screws and heat inserts are used through out to uphold the structure.
